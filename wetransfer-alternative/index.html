04 · Questions

Questions

Is RoarTransfer a free WeTransfer alternative?

Yes. You can send up to 5 GB in one transfer for free, with no monthly limit, by email or as a link, with a password and an email when it is downloaded. Without an account you can send up to 1 GB.

Is there a monthly limit like on WeTransfer Free?

No. A free RoarTransfer account has no 30-day cap. Without an account, you can send 3 transfers a day.

Are my files used to train AI?

Never. Your files are stored only to be delivered to the people with the link, for the time you chose, and then erased. Our Terms of Service say so in plain words.

Does the person receiving need an account?

No. They open the link and download. On an iPhone they can save videos and photos straight to Photos.

How long are files kept?

1 day for transfers up to 5 GB, or 3 days for transfers up to 3 GB. Then the link stops and the files are erased. You can delete them sooner from My transfers.

Who makes RoarTransfer?

The Mad Lions, a film production house in Athens, Greece. We built it for our own work and opened it to everyone.
