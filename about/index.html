Is RoarTransfer really free?

Yes. Up to 5 GB a transfer with a free account, with no ads. Pro is for people who send every day.

Does the person I send to need an account?

No. They open the link and download, on any phone or computer.

How long do files stay?

As long as you choose: 1, 3 or 7 days free, up to 30 days with Pro. Then they are erased.

Yes. In My transfers every upload is one row with its link: copy it, or send it by email to anyone.

Only people with the link, and the password if you set one. We never look at them or sell them.
