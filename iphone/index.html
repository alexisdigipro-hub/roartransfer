03 · Questions

Questions

How do I send a video to an iPhone so it goes to Photos?

Send it with RoarTransfer. When the person opens the link on an iPhone, the page shows Save to Photos: one tap prepares the files, a second tap opens the share sheet, and Save Video puts it in the Photos app in full quality.

Why do downloaded videos end up in Files on an iPhone?

Safari saves downloads to the Files app. A web page cannot write to Photos by itself, so RoarTransfer hands the files to the iPhone’s share sheet, which can.

Is the video compressed?

No. It arrives exactly as you sent it, in its original quality and name.

Which files can go to Photos?

Videos in MP4, MOV and M4V, and photos in JPG, HEIC, PNG, GIF and WebP, up to 2 GB together. Bigger transfers or other files download to Files as usual.

Does it work on an iPad?

Yes, the same way as on an iPhone.

Does the person need an app or an account?

No. They only need the link and Safari.
