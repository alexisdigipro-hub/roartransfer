Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

RoarTransfer Studio (studio.roartransfer.com) is run by The Mad Lions, a film production house in Athens, Greece (“we”). It is where people review and deliver sound, video and pictures. This page says, in plain words, what we keep, why, for how long, who helps us run it and what you can ask of us. Questions: lab@themadlions.com.

What we keep

  • Your Roar Account: your email, the name and picture you choose, and a sign-in session. It is the same account as the other Roar apps (RoarMe, RoarTransfer, RoarWriter…).
  • What you put in a project: the files you upload (sound, video, pictures, stems), their versions, the facts the page reads from them (length, size, loudness, peaks, waveform, a small preview frame), project covers, credits and lyrics, folders and playlists.
  • Notes: what you write, the second or stretch it is about, the spot on a picture, a drawing on a video frame, and spoken notes (a short recording you make with your microphone, only when you press it).
  • Stills and picks: frames you grab from a video, and the hearts you put on pictures.
  • Your notepad: private, only you see it.
  • Your studio and settings: studio name, logo, banner and background, what new links start with, which emails you want.
  • People and links: who is in a project and with which role; the share links you make, with their end date and what they allow. A link password is kept only as a one-way hash.
  • People on a link do not need an account. If they write a note, approve, pick or grab a still, we keep the name they type with it.
  • Activity: notes, uploads, approvals, finals and downloads, and that a link was opened or played, with the name given (never an IP address in this list), so the bell and the emails can tell you what happened. This activity is deleted after 30 days.

In your browser

We use your browser’s own storage (not advertising cookies) for your sign-in, your theme, background, language, tips you closed, the frame rate of videos you opened and the name you gave on a link. Nothing here is used to track you across other sites.

Why

To run the service you asked for: store your files, show them to the people you choose, keep notes and versions, send the notifications you turned on. We do not sell or rent anything about you, and we do not use your files or notes to train AI.

Who helps us run it

  • Supabase: accounts and the database (projects, notes, settings).
  • Cloudflare (R2): the files themselves (uploads, stills, spoken notes, stems), and the studio.roartransfer.com address.
  • Resend: the notification emails, only if you have them on.
  • GitHub Pages serves the page; fonts come from Google Fonts and one library from jsDelivr, which see your IP address as any website does.

For how long

Your files and notes stay until you delete them. Deleting a version, a file or a project deletes its files from storage and its data from the database, stills, stems and spoken notes included. A link stops working on its end date or when you delete it. If you delete your Roar Account, your projects go with it.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete what we keep about you, or object to how we use it. Write to lab@themadlions.com and we answer within a month. You can also complain to the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (dpa.gr).

Children

RoarTransfer Studio is for people aged 16 and over.

Changes

If this page changes in a way that matters, we say so in the app before it applies.

The people you share a project or a link with can see, play and (if you allow it) download what you share with them. Share links only with people you trust.

Τι κρατάμε

  • Το Roar Account σου: το email σου, το όνομα και τη φωτογραφία που διαλέγεις, και τη σύνδεσή σου. Είναι ο ίδιος λογαριασμός με τις άλλες Roar εφαρμογές (RoarMe, RoarTransfer, RoarWriter…).
  • Ό,τι βάζεις σε ένα project: τα αρχεία που ανεβάζεις (ήχος, βίντεο, φωτογραφίες, stems), τις εκδοχές τους, τα στοιχεία που διαβάζει η σελίδα από αυτά (διάρκεια, μέγεθος, ένταση, peaks, κυματομορφή, ένα μικρό καρέ προεπισκόπησης), εξώφυλλα, συντελεστές και στίχους, φακέλους και playlists.
  • Σημειώσεις: ό,τι γράφεις, το δευτερόλεπτο ή το διάστημα που αφορά, το σημείο σε μια φωτογραφία, μια ζωγραφιά πάνω σε καρέ βίντεο, και φωνητικές σημειώσεις (μια σύντομη ηχογράφηση με το μικρόφωνό σου, μόνο όταν το πατάς).
  • Καρέ και επιλογές: τα καρέ που κρατάς από ένα βίντεο και τις καρδιές στις φωτογραφίες.
  • Το σημειωματάριό σου: ιδιωτικό, το βλέπεις μόνο εσύ.
  • Το στούντιό σου και οι ρυθμίσεις: όνομα στούντιο, λογότυπο, banner και φόντο, με τι ξεκινάνε τα νέα links, ποια email θέλεις.
  • Άνθρωποι και links: ποιος είναι σε ένα project και με ποιον ρόλο· τα links που φτιάχνεις, με τη λήξη τους και τι επιτρέπουν. Ο κωδικός ενός link κρατιέται μόνο ως μονόδρομο hash.
  • Όσοι μπαίνουν από link δεν χρειάζονται λογαριασμό. Αν γράψουν σημείωση, εγκρίνουν, διαλέξουν ή κρατήσουν καρέ, κρατάμε το όνομα που γράφουν μαζί του.
  • Δραστηριότητα: σημειώσεις, ανεβάσματα, εγκρίσεις, τελικά και λήψεις, και ότι ένα link άνοιξε ή έπαιξε, με το όνομα που δόθηκε (ποτέ διεύθυνση IP σε αυτή τη λίστα), ώστε το καμπανάκι και τα email να σου λένε τι έγινε. Σβήνεται μετά από 30 μέρες.
